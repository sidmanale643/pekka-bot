Gmail

Read, search, send and reply to your email.

Loading…+

Sign in with Google to connect your Gmail account. Connecting leaves Pekka access off until you enable it below. When enabled, bots can read and search your mail, including PDF and image attachments, send and reply as you, save drafts and change labels such as read, archived and starred. They cannot permanently delete mail.

Server setup required

Configure GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID, GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET, GOOGLE_REDIRECT_URI and PEKKA_PLUGIN_KEY on the Pekka server, then refresh.

Open a bot and ask it to summarise unread mail, find a message, reply to a thread or draft an email. Reconnecting turns access off until you enable it again. Disconnect revokes Pekka's access at Google and removes the saved connection.

How it works

Each bot has a description, a sandbox and a memory. It can learn your preferences and refine its purpose as you chat.

How do I get started?

Start chatting with Chief of Staff, your primary bot. Tell it about yourself and what you want help with, or give it a task straight away. You can also create a bot with a name and description for a particular kind of work.

What does the chief of staff do?

Chief of Staff is listed first and manages your other bots. It handles general requests itself and hands work to the bot whose purpose fits, briefing several at once when that helps. It can create a new bot when you ask, change other bots' descriptions and instructions, and schedule work for any of them. You can rename it, but not delete it.

What does the computer view show?

Open it with the monitor button above a chat. While the bot works it shows each command it runs on its computer, with the output and exit code, and the files it reads, saves or edits. Sandbox sessions are ephemeral: once a task ends its session is shut down, and the view only shows tasks run while this page is open.

What does a bot remember?

Open the bot panel with the button at the top right of a chat, then Memory, to read and edit its preferences and knowledge. Conversation history is saved with your account and is separate from bot memory. To start a chat over, open the bot panel and choose Clear chat; its memory is kept.

How do scheduled tasks work?

Open Scheduled to create a task for a bot, or ask a bot to schedule one. From there you can pause, resume or cancel tasks and read the latest result. The scheduler (pnpm pekka scheduler) must be running for scheduled tasks to execute.

Where are my settings saved?

Your profile and preferences stay in this browser and do not sync between devices. Chat history is saved with your account, so it shows on every device. Export it from Settings to keep a copy.
