Productivity ↗

Gmail

Read, search, send and reply to your email.

Loading…+

Sign in with Google to connect your Gmail account. Connecting leaves Pekka access off until you enable it below. When enabled, bots can read and search your mail, send and reply as you, save drafts and change labels such as read, archived and starred. They cannot permanently delete mail.

Server setup required

Configure GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID, GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET, GOOGLE_REDIRECT_URI and PEKKA_PLUGIN_KEY on the Pekka server, then refresh.

Open a bot and ask it to summarise unread mail, find a message, reply to a thread or draft an email. Reconnecting turns access off until you enable it again. Disconnect revokes Pekka's access at Google and removes the saved connection.

Notion

Find, read and add to your team's pages and notes.

Loading…+

Choose the pages to share on Notion's consent screen. Connecting leaves Pekka access off until you enable it below. When enabled, bots can search and read shared content, create pages and append text within the connection's permissions.

Server setup required

Configure NOTION_CLIENT_ID, NOTION_CLIENT_SECRET, NOTION_REDIRECT_URI and PEKKA_PLUGIN_KEY on the Pekka server, then refresh.

Open a bot and ask it to find or read a shared Notion page, create a page or add notes. Reconnect to choose shared pages again on Notion's consent screen; access stays off after reconnecting until you enable it. Disconnect removes this workspace's saved connection.

Developer tools ↗

GitHub

Explore repositories, manage issues and create pull requests.

Loading…+

Authorize your GitHub account with repository access. The requested scope allows reading and writing repositories your account can access, subject to organization approval. Connecting leaves Pekka access off until you enable it below. Bots can read repositories, issues and pull requests, create issues and pull requests, and add comments.

Server setup required

Configure GITHUB_CLIENT_ID, GITHUB_CLIENT_SECRET, GITHUB_REDIRECT_URI and PEKKA_PLUGIN_KEY on the Pekka server, then refresh.

Ask a bot to inspect a repository, read an issue or pull request, open an issue, add a comment or create a pull request. GitHub tools cannot merge pull requests or delete repositories. Reconnecting keeps access off until you enable it. Disconnect removes your saved connection.

How it works

Each bot has a description, a sandbox and a memory. It can learn your preferences and refine its purpose as you chat.

How do I get started?

Create a bot with a name and description. Start chatting to tell it about yourself and what you want help with, or give it a task straight away.

What does a bot remember?

Open Details, then Memory, to read and edit its preferences and knowledge. Conversation history is kept in this browser and is separate from bot memory.

How do scheduled tasks work?

Open Scheduled to create a task for a bot, or ask a bot to schedule one. From there you can pause, resume or cancel tasks and read the latest result. The scheduler (pnpm pekka scheduler) must be running for scheduled tasks to execute.

Where are my settings saved?

Your profile, preferences and history stay in this browser and do not sync between devices. Export your history from Settings to keep a copy.
